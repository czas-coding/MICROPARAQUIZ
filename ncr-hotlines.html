🚨 National Capital Region (NCR) Emergency Directory

Direct contact lines for emergency response, patient referral, ambulance dispatch, and apex trauma hospitals across Metro Manila.

Emergency 911 (NCR / Nationwide)
General police, ambulance dispatch, fire, and disaster response.
📞 Call 911
Philippine Red Cross EMS
Emergency medical dispatch, ambulance transport, and blood bank inquiries.
📞 Dial 143 or (02) 8790-2300
DOH Hospital Referral (One Hospital Command)
National patient coordination, bed availability, and hospital transfer.
📞 Dial 1555 or (02) 8865-0500
UP-Philippine General Hospital (PGH)
Ermita, Manila • National University Hospital & Tertiary Emergency Care.
📞 Call (02) 8554-8400
National Center for Mental Health (NCMH)
24/7 Crisis line for psychological support and suicide prevention.
📞 Dial 1553 or 0917-899-8727
MMDA Emergency Rescue
Metro Manila flood rescue, road crashes, and roadside medical emergencies.
📞 Call 136
