Privacy Policy & Cookie Disclosures

TranslaNurse is dedicated to the ethical advancement of public health literacy. Because health inquiries involve sensitive personal context, our architecture is engineered with zero server-side retention and strict client-side data sovereignty.

1. Non-Commercial Intent

TranslaNurse is strictly an educational tool. We do not monetize user queries, display commercial advertisements, or broker clinical records to pharmaceutical corporations, insurance underwriters, or data aggregators.

2. What Information We Process

  • Clinical & Symptom Input: Text typed into the workspace is processed temporarily via encrypted API calls to generate plain-language explanations.
  • Laboratory Documents & Images: Camera snapshots or PDF uploads are processed in active browser memory and are not stored in any external database.
  • Local Storage (Client-Side Only): Your consult history is preserved exclusively in your local device storage (`localStorage`). You can wipe this data anytime by clicking "Clear All" in the History Drawer.

3. Cookie Policy

TranslaNurse uses no commercial tracking cookies or behavioral advertising identifiers. The only local storage tokens utilized are functional preferences: saving your light/dark mode selection (`translanurse_theme`) and temporary consultation logs on your device.

4. Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) Compliance

In accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines), TranslaNurse upholds transparency, legitimate educational purpose, and proportional data handling. Because we do not collect personal identities or retain records on remote databases, your personal health information remains entirely in your custody.
