📚 5-Pillar Clinical Sourcing Methodology

Every reference range, symptom explanation, and first-aid boundary in TranslaNurse is cross-verified against five tiers of peer-reviewed clinical and public health institutions.

PILLAR 01

Global & Government Health Authorities

World Health Organization (WHO), Republic of the Philippines Department of Health (DOH) Clinical Practice Guidelines, U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

PILLAR 02

Elite Academic Medical Centers

Mayo Clinic Patient Care & Health Information, Cleveland Clinic Health Library, Johns Hopkins Medicine Health Library, and Harvard Health Publishing (Harvard Medical School).

PILLAR 03

Specialized Professional Associations & Manuals

MSD Manuals (Consumer & Professional Editions), American Heart Association (AHA), American Diabetes Association (ADA), and American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons (AAOS OrthoInfo).

PILLAR 04

Consumer Portals & Lab Testing Directories

MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine) and Testing.com (formerly Lab Tests Online, peer-reviewed by the Association for Diagnostics & Laboratory Medicine / ADLM).

PILLAR 05

Point-of-Care & Professional Portals (Open-Access Sections)

NCBI Bookshelf StatPearls (Open-Access Clinical Reviews), UpToDate Patient Education (Beyond the Basics Open Access), and BMJ Best Practice Patient Leaflets.

🚫 STRICT SOURCE EXCLUSION POLICY: TranslaNurse never cites or extracts medical guidance from Wikipedia, Scribd, unverified blogs, crowd-edited wikis, or commercial supplement portals.
