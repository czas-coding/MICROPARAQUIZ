Terms of Service & Usage Terms

By accessing or interacting with TranslaNurse, you acknowledge and agree to the non-commercial clinical terms stated below.

1. Educational & Triage Literacy Scope

TranslaNurse is created for patient empowerment and health literacy. All outputs—including normal range comparisons, language translations, child-friendly analogies, and non-pharmacological comfort steps—are designed to inform patient-doctor communication, not replace it.

2. Emergency Exclusions

If you or someone in your care is experiencing life-threatening symptoms—such as crushing chest pain, acute shortness of breath, sudden numbness or facial droop, severe uncontrolled bleeding, or suicidal distress—do not wait for an app response. Dial 911, contact the Philippine Red Cross (143), or go directly to the nearest hospital Emergency Department.

3. Non-Pharmacological Boundary

TranslaNurse maintains a strict Zero-Medication Protocol. The platform will never prescribe, recommend changes to, or calculate dosages for prescription medications or over-the-counter pharmaceuticals. All medication decisions belong exclusively to licensed prescribing physicians.
