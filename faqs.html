✦ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About TranslaNurse

TranslaNurse is a non-commercial, open-access clinical literacy translator designed to bridge health communication gaps for patients and caregivers across the Philippines and Asia.

01. What is TranslaNurse and who built it?

TranslaNurse is a student-led, non-commercial health advocacy initiative created by nursing students at Mapúa University. Its mission is to convert complex laboratory results, clinical notes, and symptom fears into plain-language, culturally empathetic summaries tailored to everyday reading levels.

02. Why does TranslaNurse refuse to suggest medicines?

Zero-Medication Protocol: Patient safety is our highest ethical priority. Taking the wrong drug, improper dosage, or self-medicating can mask serious illnesses, induce drug-drug interactions, or trigger organ injury. TranslaNurse provides strictly non-pharmacological comfort measures, symptom explanations, and red-flag limits, while prompting patients to seek verified medical consultation for all prescriptions.

03. How does the lab sheet scanner (OCR) work?

You can drop, upload, or paste (Ctrl+V) photos or PDFs of standard laboratory forms (e.g., CBC, Fasting Blood Sugar, Serum Creatinine, Urinalysis, Lipid Panels). The tool extracts individual analyte lines and correlates them against validated clinical reference ranges, flagging results as Normal, High, or Low.

04. Which Philippine languages are supported?

We believe medical understanding should never depend on English fluency. TranslaNurse supports 10 major Philippine languages: Tagalog, Cebuano (Bisaya), Ilocano, Pangasinan / Pangasinense, Hiligaynon (Ilonggo), Chavacano, Kapampangan, Waray, Bicolano, and Maguindanaon.

05. Can TranslaNurse diagnose my condition?

No. TranslaNurse cannot perform in-person physical inspections, auscultation (listening to lungs/heart), palpation, or official radiological interpretations. It is strictly a health literacy companion that explains terminology and prepares you for productive consultations with licensed physicians.

06. Where are my lab results and text queries stored?

Your data is stored locally on your own device via browser memory (`localStorage`). TranslaNurse operates without user profiling, tracking cookies, or commercial databases. Your clinical data is never sold or retained on external servers.
